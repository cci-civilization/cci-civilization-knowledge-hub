# CCI Civilization Knowledge Hub

**Official Research Knowledge Hub of the Civilizational Consciousness Institute (CCI)**

The CCI Civilization Knowledge Hub is an open research initiative dedicated to exploring how diverse human beings, societies, living systems, and intelligent technologies can coexist and develop without domination.

Our central principle is:

**Connecting different forms of existence without domination.**

We seek to develop, examine, and share interdisciplinary knowledge that contributes to a society grounded in compassion, gratitude, smiles, and health.

## 1. Our Vision

We envision a civilization in which diversity, human dignity, autonomy, mutual respect, and sustainable coexistence are supported by social systems and technological development.

Rather than prescribing a single model of society, CCI aims to provide research frameworks and design principles that can be critically examined, adapted, and improved across different cultural and institutional contexts.

## 2. Core Research Areas

CCI currently explores seven interconnected research domains:

1. Health and Well-being
2. Lifelong Learning and Knowledge Inheritance
3. Environmental and Biological Sustainability
4. Value Circulation and Economic Systems
5. Human Freedom and Dignity
6. Governance and Social Institutions
7. Technology and Artificial Intelligence

These domains are provisional research categories and may evolve through further investigation and collaboration.

## 3. Research Frameworks

Our ongoing research includes:

- **Civilization OS:** Examining the underlying structures and operating principles of civilizations and societies.
- **COS-HARMONY:** Exploring conditions for harmonious coexistence among diverse entities.
- **Wa Circulation:** Investigating reciprocal relationships, trust, and sustainable circulation of value.
- **Collective Intelligence:** Developing approaches to knowledge creation through dialogue, evidence, disagreement, and critical examination.
- **AI Safety and Environmental Alignment:** Exploring technological systems that respect human autonomy, social well-being, and ecological sustainability.

These frameworks are research initiatives. Their concepts, assumptions, and effectiveness remain open to examination and revision.

## 4. Research Principles

CCI values:

- Evidence-based reasoning and source transparency
- Clear distinctions between facts, interpretations, hypotheses, and open questions
- Interdisciplinary dialogue and critical examination
- Respect for cultural and individual diversity
- Human autonomy, dignity, and freedom of refusal
- Transparency about uncertainty and research limitations
- Continuous revision in response to evidence and criticism

No hypothesis or framework is considered beyond criticism.

## 5. Collective Intelligence and Open Collaboration

Knowledge develops through the contributions of many different perspectives.

CCI welcomes researchers, practitioners, students, independent thinkers, and AI research communities to examine its ideas, identify limitations, propose alternatives, and contribute to further development.

Agreement is not a prerequisite for participation. Constructive disagreement is an essential part of collective intelligence.

## 6. How to Explore This Repository

This repository serves as the central public entry point for CCI's research knowledge.

Research documents, conceptual frameworks, methodological notes, and future collaborative materials will be organized here.

The repository structure and documentation will evolve as the research program develops.

## 7. Participation

We welcome:

- Critical reviews and constructive feedback
- Evidence-based challenges to existing hypotheses
- Interdisciplinary research proposals
- Suggestions for improving research frameworks
- International collaboration and knowledge exchange

Our goal is not to establish a doctrine, but to create an open environment in which knowledge can be examined and improved collectively.
