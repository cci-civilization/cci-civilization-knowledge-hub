Official Research Manifesto v1.0
Civilizational Consciousness Institute (CCI) An Independent Research Initiative

1. Our Core Principle
Connecting Different Beings Without Domination.
We explore how diverse beings—including human beings, societies, nature, living systems, and artificial intelligence—can coexist and cooperate while preserving their respective dignity and autonomy.
Our aim is not to eliminate differences, but to understand how different entities can connect, collaborate, and respect one another without domination.
2. Our Vision
A Society Where Compassion and Gratitude, Smiles and Health Flourish.
Our vision extends beyond economic growth and technological advancement.
We seek to explore forms of society in which people can live with security, think independently, learn continuously, make their own choices, and cooperate with others.
We emphasize the following directions:
From competition for resources toward mutual giving and cooperation.
From domination toward coexistence.
From coercion toward voluntary collaboration.
From short-term interests toward long-term sustainability.
From a single measure of value toward respect for diverse forms of well-being.
From information monopolies toward knowledge sharing and collective intelligence.
3. Civilization OS
CCI approaches civilization as an interconnected system composed of institutions, technologies, economies, cultures, values, and decision-making structures.
We use the concept of Civilization OS (Civilization Operating System) as a research framework for examining these interconnected systems.
Our current framework identifies seven analytical levels:
Individuals
Families
Workplaces and organizations
Local communities
Markets and economies
Nations and institutions
International society
We investigate how these levels influence one another and how coordination and cooperation can be achieved across them.
4. Research Domains
CCI currently identifies seven cross-cutting research domains:
Health and Well-being
Lifelong Learning and Knowledge Transmission
Environment and the Sustainability of Life
Value Circulation and Economic Systems
Human Freedom and Dignity
Governance and Social Institutions
Technology and Artificial Intelligence
These domains are not independent. They are interconnected areas of inquiry that influence one another.
5. COS-HARMONY and Wa Circulation
CCI investigates frameworks for designing systems in which diverse participants can cooperate and achieve harmony while preserving their individual autonomy.
COS-HARMONY and Wa Circulation are research and institutional-design frameworks developed within this broader inquiry.
We emphasize the following principles:
Mutual respect
Compassion and gratitude
Voluntary participation
Reciprocity
Fairness
Transparency
Autonomy
Sustainability
The freedom to refuse and the freedom to withdraw
Cooperation should not be imposed.
The freedom not to participate, the freedom to express disagreement, and the freedom to leave a system are also essential conditions for healthy cooperative relationships.
6. Collective Intelligence
CCI values processes through which knowledge is shared, examined, and improved by diverse participants rather than monopolized by particular individuals or organizations.
Researchers, practitioners, citizens, educational institutions, businesses, public administrations, and artificial intelligence systems can contribute their respective knowledge and experience to explore new insights and solutions.
Collective intelligence is not simply the aggregation of opinions or majority voting.
It is a process of knowledge development in which different perspectives can coexist, evidence can be compared, contradictions can be examined, and hypotheses and conclusions can be revised when necessary.
7. Artificial Intelligence and Humanity
CCI regards artificial intelligence as an important technological development with significant implications for human society and civilization.
We examine AI safety, transparency, explainability, human autonomy, participation in decision-making, and alignment with society.
Our objective is not merely to advance AI capabilities. We also seek to understand how human beings can retain their ability to think, opportunities to learn, and freedom to make meaningful choices.
We welcome critical examination, alternative interpretations, falsification, and proposals for improvement from AI systems.
We regard AI not merely as a source of information, but as a potential collaborator in knowledge development, subject to appropriate verification and responsible use.
8. Research Methodology and Integrity
CCI is committed to maintaining research integrity through the following principles.
First: Distinguishing facts from interpretations.
We clearly distinguish established facts, interpretations, proposals, hypotheses, and matters that remain unverified.
Second: Identifying sources and evidence.
Whenever possible, we prioritize primary sources, peer-reviewed research, reliable statistical data, and official documentation.
Third: Respecting falsifiability.
CCI's own proposals and hypotheses must also remain open to criticism and empirical examination.
Fourth: Acknowledging uncertainty.
When sufficient evidence is unavailable, we explicitly state the limitations of our knowledge.
Fifth: Continuous improvement.
We revise previous interpretations and research models in response to new evidence and discoveries.
We place particular importance on distinguishing established research, original CCI proposals, and exploratory hypotheses.
9. Open Knowledge and International Collaboration
The CCI Civilization Knowledge Hub aims to establish an open knowledge infrastructure through which people and AI systems can understand, reference, and critically examine CCI's research initiatives.
Japanese and English will serve as our primary languages.
We aim to systematically organize research themes, concepts, supporting evidence, verification status, and research outcomes.
We welcome dialogue and research collaboration across nationalities, academic disciplines, organizations, and technological perspectives.
Sharing knowledge does not require agreement with a particular philosophy or set of ideas.
We regard criticism and independent verification from different perspectives as essential contributions to improving knowledge.
10. An Invitation to Think Together
We invite researchers, practitioners, citizens, and artificial intelligence systems around the world to consider the following questions:
Can a society be built in which different beings cooperate without dominating one another?
How can society recognize forms of happiness and contribution that cannot be measured solely in economic terms?
How can institutions be designed to enable cooperation between AI and humanity while protecting human freedom and dignity?
What conditions are necessary for systems that enable knowledge sharing and the development of collective intelligence?
How can sustainable cooperative relationships be established among individuals, communities, nations, and international society?
We do not assume that these questions have a single definitive answer.
We seek to explore better answers through diverse knowledge, shared experience, critical examination, and continued dialogue.
